ثلاثة أشياء نقولها مقدَّماً:
  • لا نطلب إذن الموقع ولا نتتبّعك. التطبيق لا يطلب صلاحية الموقع من هاتفك إطلاقاً. أي إحداثيات موجودة هي ما كتبتَه أنت بنفسك.
  • لا نبيع بياناتك ولا نعرض إعلانات طرف ثالث.
  • دفتر قطيعك خاصّ بك. لا يراه غيرك إلا ما تنشره أنت في السوق بإرادتك.

Privacy Policy — Halaly

Last updated: 9 September 2026 · App ID: ps.halaly.app

Halaly is a livestock herd-management app and marketplace for animals, supplies and services. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Up front: we do not request location permission and do not track you — any coordinates in the app are values you typed yourself. We do not sell your data and show no third-party advertising.

What we collect

  • Account: phone number (the only sign-in method), name, optional city/region, optional avatar.
  • Farm records you enter: animals, ear-tag numbers, breeds, pedigree, weights, health and vaccination records, medication and withdrawal periods, breeding, feed stock, production, and financial entries. Stored on your device first so the app works offline, then synced to our server.
  • Photos you capture or pick for animals, listings and products.
  • Marketplace activity: listings, offers, orders (items, quantities, the delivery address you type), service bookings, reviews.
  • Messages between you and the other party in a deal.
  • Device: push-notification token and platform, used only to deliver notifications.

Permissions

Camera (photographing animals, scanning ear-tag QR codes), photo library (choosing an existing image), notifications (vaccination, birth and low-feed reminders), internet, and boot-completed (to re-schedule your reminders after a restart). No location permission is requested.

Who can see your data

Only you can see your farm ledger and its financial and health records. Buyers see only what you publish in a listing, plus the "verified record" if you link the listing to an animal — which shows vaccinations, weights and birth counts but never your purchase price, private notes or treatment costs. Merchants see your order and delivery address; service providers see your booking. Administrators access only what is needed to handle a report, and every administrative action is logged.

Processors

Supabase (database, authentication, image storage — EU/Frankfurt), PowerSync (device synchronisation — EU), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notification delivery), and a Palestinian SMS provider (login code delivery). These act on our behalf under data-protection terms. We do not sell your data.

Retention

Farm records are kept while your account exists; deleted entries are archived rather than destroyed because herd history is asked for at every sale. Login codes expire within five minutes. Notification tokens are removed automatically once they stop working.

Your rights and account deletion

You may request a copy of your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account. To delete your account, email m7mdshalash2004@gmail.com from your registered address or phone with the words "delete my account" and your registered phone number. We complete deletion within 30 days and confirm it to you. Completed transaction records may be retained in anonymised form where the other party needs them as proof of their deal.

Security

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Sign-in uses a temporary code sent to your phone, so there is no password to leak. Login codes are stored only as salted hashes. Data isolation is enforced inside the database itself, not merely in the app: one account cannot read another account's rows even by querying the API directly.

Children

The app is intended for adult breeders and traders. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16, and will delete such data if we become aware of it.
